Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

1. Who we are

VAT Checker for Sheets (the "Add-on") is a Google Sheets add-on published by Arthur Bard, a sole trader established in France, 222 Place Saint-Léger, 73000 Chambéry (the "Publisher"). Contact: barterfrance@gmail.com.

2. What the Add-on does

The Add-on provides a custom function =VAT() (alias =TVA()) which takes a VAT number typed in a cell, queries the European Commission's VIES service (and, for French numbers when VIES is unavailable, the INSEE Sirene API) and writes the validity, registered name and address back into your spreadsheet.

3. Data the Add-on accesses

  • Cell contents passed to the function: only the VAT number(s) you give to =VAT(). The Add-on does not read any other cell.
  • Licence key (Pro subscribers): stored in Google Apps Script user properties, tied to your Google account, to verify your subscription.
  • Free-check counter: a single number, stored in user properties (or in document properties when Google does not make user properties available to a custom function).

The Add-on requests only the Google permissions strictly needed: access to the current spreadsheet (spreadsheets.currentonly), connection to external services (script.external_request) and display of a sidebar (script.container.ui). When installed from the Google Workspace Marketplace, Google also requests access to your email address and basic profile (userinfo.email, userinfo.profile): the Add-on does not read, store or transmit this information.

4. Data shared with third parties

  • VIES (European Commission) receives the VAT number being checked. A VAT number is a public business identifier.
  • INSEE Sirene API receives the French SIREN derived from a French VAT number, only when VIES France is unavailable.
  • Lemon Squeezy (payment provider, Merchant of Record) processes subscription payments, issues licence keys and receives the key when the Add-on validates it. The Add-on never receives your card details. Policy: lemonsqueezy.com/privacy.

No other data is shared, and no data is sold, rented or used for advertising.

5. What we do not do

We operate no server and no database: we keep neither your spreadsheets nor your Google account data, and check results are only kept in the 6-hour cache described in section 6. We do not sell, rent or share any data. We use no advertising trackers.

6. Data protection

The data handled by the Add-on, including Google user data (the cell value passed to the function), is protected by the following mechanisms:

  • Encryption in transit: all requests to VIES, the INSEE Sirene API and the Lemon Squeezy licence API use HTTPS (TLS) only. The Add-on manifest allow-lists only these three HTTPS endpoints; Google Apps Script blocks any other destination.
  • Encryption at rest: the licence key, the counter and the temporary cache are stored only on Google's infrastructure (Google Apps Script Properties and Cache services), which encrypts data at rest.
  • Isolation and access control: this data can only be read by the Add-on itself. The Publisher has no access to your spreadsheets, properties or Google credentials. Access to the Google Cloud project, the Add-on code and the Lemon Squeezy account is restricted to the Publisher's own account.
  • Data minimisation: the Add-on reads only the VAT number you pass to it and writes only to the current spreadsheet. It cannot open any other file in your Google Drive.
  • Limited cache: check results (public business register data) are cached for at most 6 hours in the Google Apps Script cache to avoid duplicate requests, then deleted automatically. The cache holds no information about who made the check.
  • Secrets: the Publisher's INSEE API key is kept in script properties, never in the code or in your spreadsheets, and is never shown to users.
  • Logs: when a technical error occurs, Google Apps Script records the error message in Google Cloud Logging, accessible only to the Publisher's account and kept for 30 days.
  • Payments: card details are entered directly on Lemon Squeezy and processed by its PCI DSS certified payment providers; they never pass through the Add-on.
  • Incident response: if a personal data breach occurs, we notify the affected users and, where the GDPR requires it, the French data protection authority (CNIL) within 72 hours.
  • In-app notice: the Add-on sidebar (Extensions › VAT Checker for Sheets › Activate licence / My account) states which data is sent and to whom, links to this policy and lets you delete your data.

7. Google user data

The Add-on's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google data is used solely to provide the function described above.

8. Artificial intelligence

Data obtained through Google Workspace APIs is not used to develop, improve or train generalized or non-personalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models. The Add-on sends no data to any AI service.

9. Retention and deletion

The licence key and counter remain in your Apps Script properties, on Google's infrastructure, until you delete them. To erase them, open the sidebar (Extensions › VAT Checker for Sheets › Activate licence / My account) and click "Delete my data". The Publisher cannot access this data, which is why deletion is done from the Add-on. You can also revoke the Add-on's access to your account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Cached results expire within 6 hours. Billing data is retained by Lemon Squeezy according to its legal obligations; to request its deletion, e-mail us. Step-by-step help is on the help page.

10. Your rights (GDPR)

You have the right to access, rectify, erase and object. Write to barterfrance@gmail.com. You may lodge a complaint with the CNIL (cnil.fr) or your local data protection authority.

11. Changes

Any change will be published on this page with its date. If the Add-on's use of Google data ever changes, you will be told in the Add-on and asked to agree before any new use.
