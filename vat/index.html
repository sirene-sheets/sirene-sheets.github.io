Frequently asked questions

Where does the data come from?

From VIES, the European Commission's VAT Information Exchange System, queried live. For French numbers, the INSEE Sirene register is used as a fallback when VIES France is unavailable.

Is my spreadsheet data stored?

No. The add-on reads only the VAT number in the cell you pass to the formula, sends it to VIES, and writes the answer back. Nothing else is read or stored. See the privacy policy.

Which countries are supported?

All 27 EU member states plus Northern Ireland (XI). UK numbers (GB) are not supported yet.

Why does Germany return no name?

Some member states (Germany, Spain for individuals…) do not publish the registered name through VIES. The validity check still works.

Is this proof for the tax office?

The result shows the date of the check. For a VIES consultation number, use the official VIES website; this add-on is a bulk-checking convenience, not a legal archive.

How do I cancel?

From the link in your subscription e-mail, in one click. No commitment.
